I am a Ph.D. Student at KAIST AI, advised by Prof. Han-Lim Choi in the Laboratory for Information and Control Systems (LiCS). My research interests include vision-language models, multi-agent systems, and intelligent robotics. I am particularly interested in how robot swarms coordinate and make decentralized decisions under limited sensing, communication, and computation. My long-term interests also include superintelligence and how learning and reasoning can enable more capable autonomous systems.
